These 411 flags are supported by Ares but NOT by vanilla
RA2 or YR — a mod must load Ares/Antares for them to work. If a user asks for
one of these, tell them it needs Ares/Antares.
Source: ModENC imageusage (Game_ares_yes.png) minus the YR and RA2 sets.
